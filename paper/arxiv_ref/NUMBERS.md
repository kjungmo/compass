# NUMBERS — claim-to-artifact map for `paper/arxiv_ref`

Every quantitative claim in the re-typeset manuscript is listed below with the
committed artifact (file and field) or closed-form derivation it comes from.
`python3 paper/arxiv_ref/tools/check_numbers.py` verifies each row: the
"Printed" TeX literal must occur in the flattened manuscript, and the "Check"
column is re-executed (`lit:` literal present in the artifact, `csv:` statistic
recomputed from `ablation_raw.csv` as variant/scenario/metric/stat=value,
`yaml:` knob in `src/compass_nav2/config/compass_params.yaml`, `calc:` closed-form
derivation from the stated knobs, `table:` every cell checked against the raw CSV).
The same tool also runs the repository guard `scripts/check_paper_numbers.py`
against the LaTeX text. Data-bearing tables and pgfplots figures are generated by
`paper/arxiv_ref/tools/gen_tables_figures.py` from the same artifacts.

Conceptual figures (`fig_class_lifecycle.pdf`, `fig_decision_flow.pdf`,
`fig_social_cost_field.pdf`) are schematic vector PDFs copied unchanged from
`paper/arxiv/figures/` (generated by `paper/figures/scripts/`); they carry no
measured values.
