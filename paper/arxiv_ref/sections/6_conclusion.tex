\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:concl}

This paper proposes \sname, a decision-layer planner for social navigation. It commits to a passing side with a leaky evidence accumulator --- a rectified, leaky variant of Page's one-sided CUSUM statistic~\citep{page1954cusum} with a progress-dependent threshold --- under a lexicographic safety override of the standard priority-arbitration kind. Passing classes are bound to person track IDs, a design contract that the current implementation only partly realizes.

The anti-oscillation guarantee we claim is P2, a deterministic, pathwise lower bound $N_{\min}$ on the number of control cycles between discretionary switches for fixed $dt$, under zero post-switch evidence and the range bound $D_k\le D_{\max}$ (only the accumulated-time form is claimed for measured intervals; \cref{rem:vardt}), informative under the declared condition $E_0 > (D_{\max}-\Dfloor)\,dt$. It is paired with the conditional response-time bound of \cref{prop:p2live}. P1 refines P2 to an exponentially small per-cycle switching probability and per-unit-time switching rate for i.i.d., zero-mean challenger advantages. The reachability-headroom condition $\Eremax > E_0$ is a declared design condition, not a liveness guarantee. Absent safety intervention and under the bounded-advantage, progress-rate and containment conditions of P4, discretionary reversal is blocked whenever $\rho>\bar\rho$; completion additionally needs $B<L_{\text{plan}}$ and forward invariance.

In the offline harness, immediate argmin changes its decision about 98 times per encounter in the ambiguous-tie scenario, whereas \sname\ and the 0.6~s simple dwell both record 0; the two rules differ in the transient-spike, mid-reversal and intermittent scenarios. Removing hysteresis or progress hardening alone produced no regression at the scripted perturbation levels. Decision-core latency at $K=3$ is p99 58.6~$\mu$s and max 651.3~$\mu$s. All comparators are internal. The scope of P1--P5, the short guaranteed P2 separation, the missing consistency-weight, opinion-dynamics and closed-loop comparisons, and the other limits are stated in \cref{sec:disc} and \cref{app:planned}.
